About this map

Your 24/7 distributor of credible local news.

Pinas 2026 replaces place labels with the stories that made them matter. Every glowing marker is a news event — tap one to read a summary and jump to the original reporting. Markers are coloured by how fresh the story is, so the map shows you where the country is loudest right now.

A rolling one-week window

This is a guide to what's happening now, not an archive. The map holds the last 7 days of news — browse it by Today, Last 3 days, or This week. Older stories roll off automatically, which keeps the map fast and always current.

Where the news comes from

Stories arrive two ways, and the map always tells you which:

  • ✔ Verified Hand-researched and cross-checked against multiple outlets, with a written summary.
  • ◈ Auto The top 3 headlines every 6 hours — 12 a day — pulled from an allowlist of established Philippine newsrooms. The headline and summary are the outlet's own words — never rewritten, never generated — and always link back to the original article.

Deliberately a small number: a handful of genuine headlines beats a flood of them. Automated stories are only placed on the map when the report clearly names a Philippine location. If a location can't be determined, the story is skipped rather than guessed.
